## 📊 Featured Projects

### 🏥 [AI-Powered Prediction of Hospital Length of Stay](https://github.com/ramaseshu-Data/LOS-AI-Project) (MSc Dissertation)
- Predicted length of stay for **100,000 admissions** in Microsoft's public hospital dataset, comparing Linear Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting and a neural network (ANN)
- The best model explained **96% of the variation** in length of stay (R² 0.96, typical error under half a day)
- Identified and removed a **data-leakage** column that would have produced unrealistically perfect results
- Simulated bed planning: model-based forecasts cut the daily bed-count error by **about 60%** compared with planning on average length of stay
- Tools: Python, pandas, scikit-learn, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter
